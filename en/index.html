Parent guide

Tape or pants?

Tape is ideal for newborns and lying-down changes. Pants are easier once your baby crawls or walks.

When to size up?

Red marks on thighs, tapes hard to close, or repeated leaks can signal it's time for the next size.

Leak-prevention tips

Pull the leg cuffs outward, fasten tapes evenly, and change regularly.
