Parent guide

Tape or pants?

Tape diapers are ideal for newborns and lying-down changes. Pants are easier once your baby crawls, walks or wriggles during changes.

When to size up?

Red marks on thighs or waist, tapes that are hard to close, repeated leaks, or weight above the size range are signs it may be time for the next size.

Leak-prevention tips

Pull the leg cuffs outward, fasten tapes evenly, choose the size by weight, and change regularly — especially before sleep.

What if my baby is between sizes?

It is usually best to choose the larger size when the weight is near the top of the smaller size range.

How often should I change the diaper?

Newborns need frequent changes; in general change whenever the diaper is wet or soiled. Ask your paediatrician about any health concern.
